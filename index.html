About

Engineer, linguist, systems person.

I'm a software development engineer at Amazon on Kindle Search, a service used by roughly 30 million people a month. Lately that has meant LLM-powered natural language search on Bedrock — intent classification, query rewriting and suggestion generation, all grounded against catalog metadata.

The half of that work I care most about is making it trustworthy: a golden-set regression benchmark with human relevance grading, prompt-injection filtering, and automatic fallback to lexical search when a generation is low-confidence or slow. It took library-scoped title resolution from ~0.3 to ~0.75 F1.

Before Amazon I studied Computer Science + Linguistics at UIUC, graduating with highest honors and a Bronze Tablet, and I've since finished a Master of Computer Science there. The linguistics half was not a detour — four spoken languages and a research streak in federated learning both feed the same interest in how meaning survives a lossy channel.

  • Applied LLMs — intent classification, query rewriting, grounding, prompt-injection filtering, eval harnesses
  • Distributed backends on AWS — ECS, GraphQL gateways, asynchronous Kotlin orchestration
  • Android at scale — Jetpack Compose, accessibility compliance, reusable UI systems
